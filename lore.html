OFFICE MADNESS - DATA LOG

INCIDENT REPORT #1930 // TOP SECRET
> THE COMPANY

The events unfold in New York City, inside the headquarters of a massive Japanese corporation, Tenma Enterprise — the primary rival of Yamaha. The general public views the brand as tech for the poor, given their TVs cost only 199 dollars. At the same time, the company develops a ton of software, processors, computers, and even their own gaming consoles. Granted, the systems tanked hard on the market due to their overly complicated and convoluted hardware architecture.

> THE ACCIDENT

The disaster struck right inside the corporate building at exactly 19:30 local time. A failed top-secret experiment unleashed terrifying, unholy creatures within the office walls. A hasty staff evacuation began 30 minutes later, but they were far from saving everyone.

> THE TRAP

The building consists of 19 floors. The first floor has been placed under absolute, strict lockdown, completely cutting off the only escape route to the outside world. The remaining employees are forced to spend the night right at their workstations, surrounded by hallways filled with pure nightmare.

> THE SURVIVORS

Fortunately, two local clerks managed to survive the initial wave of chaos. Their names are James Riter and James Curtis. They lack any real combat weapons, but they have adapted to weaponizing everyday office supplies. You can inflict severe damage on the monsters even with stationeries, provided you use them correctly.
