XAVIER & THE GANG

PROJECT DOSSIER // TIMELINE ARCHIVE
> XAVIER WILSON
In 1989 (8 years old): The brains of the gang. Constantly lost his temper and screamed in ALL CAPS whenever his dumb friends interrupted his music or butchered his name.
Present Day (Adult): Just as anxious and strict. Tries to control the absurdity around him but regularly breaks down into yelling "SHUT THE FUCK UP!" when Mitchell shouts right into his ear during parties.
> MITCHELL HOPKINS
In 1989 (8 years old): A crafty little instigator with a cool pompadour haircut, who genuinely believed he was the ultimate high-roller of the American nowhere.
Present Day (Adult): Rocks a white "Jaz'n" t-shirt. The undisputed master of wild and loud flexing over the phone, capable of driving Xavier completely insane within a split second.
> ERIC JARWELL
In 1989 (8 years old): A tiny, long-haired metalhead obsessed with SLAYER right at his school desk.
Present Day (Adult): Formed into a textbook badass rocker wearing a classic red Slayer t-shirt. Blasts heavy music on MTV and couldn't care less about anyone else's arguments.
> JAY D. MATHAFUQA
In 1989 (8 years old): A laid-back kid with Maori roots and a bizarre typo in his ID, always down to join any childhood stunt.
Present Day (Adult): An unbothered rasta in a bright beanie, smoking a cigarette. The most tranquil element of any party, just flexing in the background and absolutely chilling through life.
